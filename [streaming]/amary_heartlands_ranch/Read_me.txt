ATTENTION
Availability of the installed "objectloader".
Server artifacts of version 12735 and higher.
https://runtime.fivem.net/artifacts/fivem/build_server_windows/master/

For installation, you need objectloader!
If you do not have an objectloader, you can download it here:
https://github.com/kibook/redm-objectloader

In config.lua change the value:
-- Maximum number of entities that may be spawned at the same time
Config.MaxEntities = 10000

You can also use any other XML file loader, for example RSD MAPLOADER:
https://script.redstartrp.fr/package/6728387
